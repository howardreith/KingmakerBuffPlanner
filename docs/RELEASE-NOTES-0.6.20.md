# Kingmaker Buff Planner 0.6.20

Missing beneficial buffs are back in the castable list:

- **Good Hope** for bards and other casters who know it, planned as one cast over its native 15-foot allied area. An ally under Crushing Despair has the Despair removed instead of receiving Good Hope on that cast, and the run reports exactly that.
- **Firebelly** (including from a prepared spell slot). Its granted fire breath is still never offered as a buff.
- The wizard's five **Abjuration resistance** selections (Acid, Cold, Electricity, Fire, Sonic): personal, sharing the native daily use. Re-selecting the active choice costs nothing, and the choices replace each other instead of stacking.
- **Blessing of Luck and Resolve, Mass**, **Feather Step, Mass**, **Firebrand** and **Heroic Invocation**, centred on a party member (never a ground point), plus **Enlarge** and **Reduce Person, Mass**. If the game refuses a party member as the centre, the planner refuses to plan the cast rather than guessing.

Planning is more careful with overlapping and uncertain effects. Another caster's Firebrand, or a weaker recast, no longer counts as an already active Firebrand. An ally whose distance or allegiance cannot be read is never claimed as covered. A banned caster no longer prevents a valid skip. Classic planning picks the caster whose single cast covers the most requested allies.

This is an owner-authorized pre-runtime release. This exact release has not been run in the game; the new spells were verified with offline tests against the native 2.1.7b blueprints only.
